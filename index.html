Selected work

Lambots: a GenAI assistant for 500+ engineers

LAM Research, lead business analyst, May 2025 to Jan 2026

Lambots is a RAG-based chatbot that lets engineers ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from 100+ structured and unstructured data sources. I led the business analysis for it.

I wrote the functional requirements for data ingestion, embedding generation, multimodal indexing and retrieval. Because a chatbot that sounds confident and is wrong is worse than no chatbot, I also set up a benchmark testing approach with domain experts, so they could judge whether answers were accurate and relevant. After launch I tracked engagement, response quality and satisfaction, and fed what we learned back into the model.

What changed: hallucinations down 40%, engineers finding information 40% faster, and 85% user satisfaction shown on the executive dashboards I built, which then fed into roadmap decisions.
